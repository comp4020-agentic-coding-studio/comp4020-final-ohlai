# Crit 8: It's alive!

## What was the breakthrough that moved the work forward?

Letting go of my Macros app. I have put weeks into it and wanted it to be the
final project, but it is a single-user tracker on my home server, and the brief
asks for something that is better because other people are in it. Once I picked
a PT and a client, the multi-user part stopped being bolted on: the whole point
of the app is that two people see the same sets.

The second was the progress chart. Describing double progression to the agent
made me say out loud why every chart I have used gets it wrong, and the fix was
a single formula, not a feature.

## What did this work change about who I want to be as a software developer?

I want to start from the situation, not from code I already have. Reusing
Macros felt efficient, but it would have meant bending the brief around an app
built for a different question. I also noticed that the best input I gave the
agent was two screenshots of an app that already works, which was faster and
more precise than describing a UI in words.
