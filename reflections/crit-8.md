# Crit 8: It's alive!

## What was the breakthrough that moved the work forward?

Dropping Macros. I've put weeks into it and really wanted it to be the final
project, but it's a single-user tracker on my home server, and the brief wants
something that's better because other people are using it. Once I picked a PT
and a client the multi-user part stopped feeling bolted on. Two people looking
at the same sets is the whole app.

The other one was the progress chart. Explaining double progression to the
agent made me actually say why every chart I've used gets it wrong, and the fix
ended up being one formula rather than a whole feature.

## What did this work change about who I want to be as a software developer?

I want to start from the situation, not from code I already have lying around.
Reusing Macros felt efficient but I would have been bending the brief around an
app built for a different question. I also noticed the best thing I gave the
agent was two screenshots of an app that already works. That was way faster and
more precise than trying to describe a UI in words.
