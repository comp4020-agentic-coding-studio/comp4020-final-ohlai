How it works

  1. Write it. Days, exercises, sets, rep ranges and rest, the way you already program. Takes a few minutes at your desk.
  2. Send it. Your client gets one link, from your own email. They open it on their phone at the gym, no app to install.
  3. Watch it. Sets show up as they tick them off. Your dashboard shows who's gone quiet and who's stalled.

What's in it

One tap between sets

Each row is filled in from last session, so most of the time your client just lifts and ticks. No typing with sweaty hands.

Progress that makes sense

It knows about rep ranges, so going up a weight and dropping a couple of reps counts as progress, not going backwards.

Questions go to you

Clients message you from their program and can send photos for form checks. The chat is right next to the workout it's about.
