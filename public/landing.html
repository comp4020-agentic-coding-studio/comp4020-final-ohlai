How it works

  1. Write it. Days, exercises, sets, rep ranges and rest, the way you already program. Takes a few minutes at your desk.
  2. Send it. Your client gets one link, from your own email. They open it on their phone at the gym, no app to install.
  3. Watch it. Every set shows up as they tick it. Your dashboard tells you who's gone quiet, who's stalled and who's moving.

Built around how you coach

One tap between sets

Every row comes filled in from last session. Most of the time your client lifts, ticks and rests. No typing with sweaty hands.

Progress the way you read it

Rep ranges and double progression are scored properly, so going up a weight and dropping reps doesn't look like going backwards.

Questions go to you

Clients message you from the program, with photos for form checks. The chat sits next to the training it's about.
