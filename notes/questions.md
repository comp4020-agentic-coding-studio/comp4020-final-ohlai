# Questions the final docs have to answer

From crit feedback. Each one gets an answer here first, then it goes into the
README or PROCESS.

- Why does this app benefit from being real-time? Who is waiting on what, and
  how long is too long?
- Why does it benefit from being multi-user, beyond two people reading the same
  data?
- Are there other apps in this area that are real-time or multi-user? What do
  they do (Hevy, Trainerize, TrueCoach)?
- What informed each design decision? `decisions.md` is meant to make this one
  easy.
