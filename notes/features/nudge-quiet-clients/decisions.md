# nudge quiet clients: decisions

## 2026-11-08, where the nudge message is written

- What came up: the nudge needs a message the PT writes once.
- Options: write it in the dashboard popover each time; keep one saved message on the Check-ins page.
- Picked: one saved message on Check-ins. I asked for it there ("Build a custom message in the Check-ins tab").
- Why: my words; it sits next to the other thing the PT writes once for every client, the check-in questions.

## 2026-11-08, who counts as quiet (agent's call, waiting on me)

- What came up: who the nudge goes to.
- Options: (a) same rule as the dashboard's Gone quiet tile, no workout in 7+ days or never trained; (b) let the PT tick clients in the popover; (c) a different number of days.
- Went with (a), since the old preview text already said "every client who hasn't trained in 7 days" and it matches the tile. Clients who haven't signed up are skipped because chat needs an account.
- Not confirmed by me yet. Ticking clients and changing the day count are left out until I ask.

- What came up: one message to several people reads cold without their name.
- Picked: `{name}` in the message becomes the client's first name. Without it the message goes as written.
- Not confirmed by me yet.

- What came up: the old preview said "one click sends".

- Picked by me: close it. ("once you click send it should close the pop up.")
- Why: I didn't say. If the send fails the popup stays open with the error; that part was the agent's call.

The agent recommended 1: "everyone but Sam" and "only Jack" both work in one place, and you can already message one person from their client card.
- Picked by me: option 1 ("option 1").
- Why: I didn't say beyond picking it.
- This replaces the earlier "who counts as quiet" setup where Send went to every quiet client with no choice. Quiet is still worked out the same way (7+ days, or never trained).
- Still open: whether the list should show every signed-up client and not just quiet ones. Asked, not answered.

## 2026-11-09, why tick boxes for nudge by person

- Adds the reason to "nudge by person" above, which said "I didn't say beyond picking it."
- My reason: "it keep its contained without cluttering the interface with loads of nudge buttons for each person. while also giving you more control."
- So: one place for the nudge, no extra Nudge button on every client, and I still choose exactly who gets it.
